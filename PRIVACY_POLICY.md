# Privacy Policy — Afrikoba Global
Effective Date: August 22, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Afrikoba Global ("we", "our", "platform") is a pan-African digital banking platform providing VICOBA, ROSCA, P2P crowdfunding, wallet, and USSD services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share your personal information.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- Full name
- Phone number
- Email address
- National ID (NIDA) number (for KYC Level 2)
- Residential address
- Government-issued ID documents

### 2.2 Financial Information
- Wallet balances and transaction history
- VICOBA group membership and contribution records
- ROSCA pool participation
- P2P investment portfolio
- Bank/mobile money account details

### 2.3 Technical Information
- IP address
- Device information (type, OS, browser)
- Login timestamps and session data
- USSD session data

### 2.4 Usage Information
- Feature usage patterns
- Transaction amounts and frequency
- Notification preferences

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Service Delivery
- Process transactions and payments
- Manage VICOBA groups, loans, and social funds
- Facilitate ROSCA pools and payouts
- Process P2P investments and escrow
- Send OTP codes and transaction alerts

### 3.2 Security & Compliance
- Verify your identity (KYC/AML)
- Detect and prevent fraud
- Maintain audit trails for regulatory compliance
- Enforce rate limiting and security controls

### 3.3 Communication
- Send transaction notifications
- Send security alerts
- Send promotional messages (with your consent)

## 4. Data Sharing

### 4.1 Service Providers
- Beem Africa (SMS delivery)
- AzamPay (mobile money processing)
- Sentry (error monitoring)
- Hosting providers (data storage)

### 4.2 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information when required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.

### 4.3 We Never Sell Your Data
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Technical Measures
- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- Parameterized SQL queries (SQL injection prevention)
- Rate limiting and brute-force protection
- HMAC signature verification for webhooks/USSD

### 5.2 Organizational Measures
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Immutable audit logging
- Regular security assessments
- Employee access restrictions

## 6. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| User accounts | Until deletion + 30 days | Account management |
| Transaction records | 7 years | Financial regulations |
| Audit logs | 7 years | Regulatory compliance |
| OTP codes | 1 hour | Security |
| Session data | 24 hours | Security |
| Analytics events | 2 years | Service improvement |

## 7. Your Rights

### 7.1 Access
You can request a copy of your personal data via the app or by contacting us.

### 7.2 Correction
You can update your profile information at any time via the app.

### 7.3 Deletion
You can request account deletion by contacting support@afrikoba.com. We will process deletion within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

### 7.4 Data Portability
You can request your data in a machine-readable format.

### 7.5 Opt-Out
You can disable promotional notifications in Settings > Notifications.

## 8. Cookies & Tracking
Our web dashboard uses essential cookies for session management. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics that track you across websites.

## 9. Children's Privacy
Afrikoba Global is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## 10. International Data Transfers
Your data is stored in secure cloud infrastructure. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for any cross-border data transfers.

## 11. Changes to This Policy
We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification or SMS. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance.
